Privacy Policy — My Nest Interface

Effective date: October 14, 2026

What data the app accesses

My Nest Interface accesses thermostat information for my own Google Nest thermostats through the Google Smart Device Management API: current temperature, thermostat mode, and heating and cooling setpoints.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display and adjust my own thermostats. It is not used for any other purpose.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a secure credential vault on my personal automation server.

Sharing

No thermostat data is sold or shared with third parties. Google's handling of data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer at the support email listed on the app's OAuth consent screen.
